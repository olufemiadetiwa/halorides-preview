What we’re building

More than a ride. An ecosystem of opportunity.

Every journey creates an opportunity for someone: a driver earns, a fleet deploys a vehicle, a garage keeps it road-ready, a business moves its people, and a protection partner helps manage risk.

HaloRide’s ambition is to connect these activities through a more dependable mobility platform.

HaloRide
  • Drivers and fleetsVehicles, and the people who drive them.
  • Vehicle careInspection and maintenance that keep cars road-ready.
  • Insurance and protectionCover designed around the journey.
  • Passengers and businessesPeople and teams who need dependable travel.
  • Finance and energyAccess to vehicles, and the power to run them.
  • Cities and institutionsSafer, better-coordinated urban mobility.

One platform. Multiple opportunities to create value.

This is the ecosystem we are working towards. It is not a finished network.

Who we’re looking for

There’s a place for your business in what we’re building.

Whether you operate vehicles, maintain them, protect journeys or move people at scale, we’re looking to explore partnerships that make mobility work better.

Fleet owners and vehicle investors

Turn vehicle capacity into opportunity.

Who we’re looking for. Fleet operators, transport entrepreneurs, corporate fleet owners and investors with vehicles suitable for HaloRide’s service standards.

What you could gain

  • Access to platform booking opportunities
  • Fleet onboarding and driver verification processes
  • Vehicle-level trip and earnings reporting, where supported
  • Visibility into vehicle utilisation and activity
  • Opportunities to take part in service-quality initiatives

What HaloRide needs

  • Roadworthy, eligible vehicles
  • Licensed and approved drivers
  • Compliance with operating standards
  • Appropriate vehicle documentation
  • Willingness to meet safety and inspection requirements

HaloRide does not guarantee bookings, utilisation or earnings.

Partnership questions

Straight answers about what a partnership enquiry is, and what it isn’t.

Who can partner with HaloRide?

Organisations whose work touches mobility: fleets and vehicle investors, garages and service networks, insurers and insurtech providers, employers and other businesses that move people, finance, leasing and EV infrastructure providers, and public institutions. If you don’t fit a category, choose “Other Partnership” in the enquiry form.

Does sending an enquiry create a partnership?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation. Any partnership would need both sides to agree terms in writing.

Does HaloRide guarantee bookings, service volumes or earnings?

No. Nothing on this page is a guarantee of bookings, utilisation, service volumes, revenue or earnings.

Is there a founding-partner programme?

There is no formal founding-partner tier, and we are not offering exclusive territories or priority onboarding. We are inviting organisations to talk to us early, while pilots and partnership structures are still being designed.

How soon will I hear back?

We review every enquiry, but we don’t commit to a response time.

Who are HaloRide’s current partners?

We don’t name partners on this page. We would only announce a partnership once an agreement is in place and both sides have agreed to make it public.

What happens to the information I submit?

It is used to review and respond to your enquiry, as described in our Privacy Notice.

I’m an individual driver. Is this page for me?

This page is for organisations. To drive with HaloRide, start on the Drive page, and see Driver Advantage for the benefits we’re building for drivers.
