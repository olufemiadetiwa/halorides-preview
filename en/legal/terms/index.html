2. What HaloRide does

HaloRide is a technology platform that lets riders request transportation from independent drivers ("Drivers") and agree a fare with them. HaloRide does not itself provide transportation services, and Drivers are not our employees unless local law provides otherwise.

Our Fair Price feature provides an estimated fare range, a breakdown and an explanation to help riders and Drivers agree a price. Estimates are guidance based on available data and are not a guarantee of the final price, travel time or route.

3. Eligibility and your account

You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live) to create an account. Riders under that age may only travel with an account holder who is responsible for them where local rules permit.

  • You must verify a mobile phone number you control.
  • You must provide accurate information and keep it up to date.
  • You may need to complete identity steps — such as a selfie or national ID check — before certain trips, including trips at night.
  • You are responsible for activity on your account. Keep your device and one-time codes secure; HaloRide staff will never ask for your code.

4. Requesting a ride and agreeing a fare

When you request a ride you will see an estimated fair range and may submit an offer. Offers below a minimum threshold (the "fair floor") cannot be submitted. Drivers may accept your offer or propose a different price. A binding agreement for the trip is formed between you and the Driver when you select a Driver’s response.

The agreed fare is the price for the trip as booked. Changes to the destination, added stops, waiting time beyond the allowance, tolls or other charges disclosed in the app may change the final amount, and you will see any change before it is charged where practicable.

5. Payment, fees and receipts

You may pay by card, HaloRide wallet or cash where available. Card payments are processed by our payment partners; we do not store full card numbers. By adding a card you authorise us and our partners to charge it for trips, fees and amounts you owe.

Each fare includes a safety fee that funds trip monitoring. Your receipt shows the estimate, the agreed fare, a breakdown, the safety fee and HaloRide’s service fee.

If a payment fails, the amount may be recorded as an outstanding balance on your wallet, which must be settled before your next booking.
