About

Building the ride-hailing people actually trust.

HaloRide started with two problems. Riders couldn’t tell if a negotiated price was fair, and too many people didn’t feel safe getting into a stranger’s car. Drivers had it hard too: lowball offers, and passengers they knew nothing about. We set out to fix both, with pricing anyone can understand, safety on every trip and a clean-car standard we actually enforce.

Fair to both sides

Riders and drivers always see the same facts.

Safety comes first

We never trade it for speed or growth.

Built to last

Fast on everyday phones and patchy networks.
