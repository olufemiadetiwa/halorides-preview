What Trip Protection is not

It is a personal accident style benefit for the journey, and it has limits.

  • It is not comprehensive motor insurance
  • It does not replace the commercial motor insurance the law requires
  • Which parts of a journey count, from travel to pickup through to drop-off, follow the insurer’s approved definition

Driver Advantage questions

Straight answers, including where the answer is “not decided yet”.

Is Trip Protection available on every journey?

No. It is designed for eligible journeys only. Which journeys count, and from what point in the trip, will follow the insurer’s approved definition and be published before the programme starts.

Does Trip Protection replace motor insurance?

No. It is a personal accident style benefit. It is not comprehensive motor insurance, and it does not replace the commercial motor insurance the law requires.

What kinds of incidents may be covered?

Trip Protection is designed to cover accident medical expenses, accidental death and permanent disability, and income interruption after a covered injury. The final cover, limits and exclusions will be set out in the insurer’s policy, published before launch.

Who decides whether an insurance claim is paid?

The insurer. HaloRide can help you gather information and submit a claim, but the insurer assesses and decides every claim under the policy, including its limits and exclusions.

How do I earn Car Care credits?

In this preview, each qualifying paid trip adds ₦150 to your maintenance contribution, assessed over a 90-day period. The figures are subject to final commercial approval.

Can I redeem Car Care credits as cash?

No. Car Care is a contribution towards approved maintenance, paid directly to participating garages. It is not a cash balance.

What happens if I complete fewer than 200 trips?

In this preview the credit becomes usable only once you reach 200 qualifying trips within the period. What happens to credit below the threshold has not been decided.

Can I use a garage of my choice?

Car Care uses approved, participating garages. The list of garages has not been confirmed.

What if I drive a vehicle owned by a fleet partner?

A maintenance credit for a fleet-owned vehicle may mainly benefit the fleet owner, so programme terms will state who receives each benefit. Fleet arrangements may need a separate benefit for the driver, and Reward Day benefits should reach the driver through an agreed settlement.

How do I qualify for Reward Days?

Through completed paid trips and service performance, measured against published criteria. The criteria are not final.

Can I choose my Reward Day?

That has not been decided yet.

Do drivers still have safety requirements on Reward Days?

Yes. Every safety and vehicle requirement applies on a Reward Day exactly as on any other day.

Can benefit rules change?

Yes. Programme rules may change before and after launch. Final terms will be published in the programme documentation and driver terms.

When will these benefits become available?

The programme is confirmed, but we have not announced a launch date. This page will be updated when the programme’s status changes.
